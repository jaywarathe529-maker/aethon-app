# Product Management Portfolio

This directory contains three original product case-study blueprints built around technical, education, and analytics problems.

> **Status:** Discovery and product-definition stage. These documents describe proposed products, hypotheses, and execution plans. They do not claim completed launches, user interviews, or measured impact.

## Case studies

1. [Teacher Test and Evaluation Platform](./teacher-test-platform.md)
2. [Scientific Simulation Workflow Manager](./simulation-workflow-manager.md)
3. [Feature-Adoption Intelligence Dashboard](./feature-adoption-dashboard.md)

## Skills demonstrated

- Problem framing and user segmentation
- Product requirements and MVP definition
- User journeys and acceptance criteria
- RICE prioritisation and roadmap planning
- North-star, input, guardrail, and business metrics
- Experiment design and product analytics
- Go-to-market and risk analysis

## Planned validation

Each case study will be strengthened through primary user interviews, prototype testing, implementation, and measured results. Only validated findings and actual outcomes will be reported as completed work.
